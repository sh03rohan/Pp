Physics gives the rules, and CSE uses these rules to build technology. Now, look at this computer. First, electricity gives power. Then, the hardware, like the processor and memory, uses that power. ## Shammy Sultana — Electricity & Electronics

Slides 3–4 · 192 words · about 1:50

### Slide 3: Electricity and Transistors

Thank you, Habibullah. Hello, everyone. I am Shammy Sultana. Electricity is the flow of tiny charged particles called electrons. Voltage is the push, and current is the flow. It is like water in a pipe. Inside every computer chip, there are transistors. A transistor is a tiny electric switch. When the switch is ON, current flows, and we call it one. When it is OFF, no current flows, and we call it zero. Now look at these eight switches. Together, they make one byte. Zero, one, zero, zero, zero, zero, zero, one means the letter A. A modern phone chip has billions of transistors.

*Action: Click the big switch to show ON and OFF. Then point to the eight switches that make the letter A.*

### Slide 4: Logic Gates: Simple Decisions

We can connect transistors to make logic gates. A logic gate makes a simple decision. An AND gate gives one only when both inputs are one. An OR gate gives one when any input is one. A NOT gate gives the opposite. When I change the inputs, the output changes at once. Computers use millions of these gates to add numbers and make decisions. For example, a calculator uses logic gates to add two numbers. Now, Hridoy Dhar will show you how Physics helps us in real life.

*Action: Choose AND, OR, and NOT. Click input A and input B to show the output change.*

## Hridoy Dhar — Real-World Applications

Slides 5–6 · 189 words · about 1:48

### Slide 5: Light and Waves Connect Us

Thank you, Shammy. Hello, everyone. I am Hridoy Dhar. First, fiber optic cables. A fiber cable is a very thin thread of glass. It sends data as flashes of light. A flash means one, and no flash means zero. The light bounces inside the glass, so it stays inside the cable. Light is very fast, so we get fast internet. Most internet data between countries travels through these cables. Next, wireless communication. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and mobile networks use electromagnetic waves. These are invisible waves of energy, and they travel at the speed of light. Bluetooth connects close devices, like earphones. So, we can use the internet without any wires.

*Action: Click 'Send' to send the word through the cable. Then point to the Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and mobile cards.*

### Slide 6: Sensors and Robotics

Now, sensors and robots. A sensor helps a machine feel the world. It can detect movement, distance, light, and temperature. Look at this robot. It sends out a sound wave. The sound hits the box and comes back, like an echo. The robot measures the time and finds the distance. When the box is too close, the robot stops. Robot vacuum cleaners and self-driving cars use the same idea. Now, Sakibul Hasan Rohan will talk about the future of technology.

*Action: Watch the robot stop near the box. You can move the box with the slider.*

## Sakibul Hasan Rohan — Future & Conclusion

Slides 7–8 · 185 words · about 1:46

### Slide 7: Quantum Computing

Thank you, Hridoy. Hello, everyone. I am Sakibul Hasan Rohan. Let's look at the future. First, quantum computers. A normal computer uses bits. A bit is zero or one. A quantum computer uses qubits. A qubit can be in a mix of zero and one. When we measure it, we get zero or one. Quantum computers are not faster for everything. But they can help with special problems, like designing new medicines and materials. They are still new, and many need very cold temperatures. Big companies and universities are building them today.

*Action: Click 'Measure' a few times. Each time, the qubit gives 0 or 1.*

### Slide 8: The Future and Conclusion

Physics will also improve other technologies, like smart robots, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, and smaller, faster computer chips. These ideas start in Physics labs and become products for everyone. To conclude, Physics is the base of Computer Science and Engineering. Electricity powers our computers. Transistors and logic gates help them think. Light and waves connect us. Sensors help machines understand the world. Physics explains how nature works, and CSE turns that knowledge into technology. Remember this: behind every great computer, there is great Physics. Thank you for listening. Do you have any questions?
